---
Glowing tubes behind a red grille, tight lows, aggressive mids and enough gain for metal, hardcore and everything in between. Pyroclast stays defined even at high gain and sits precisely in the mix with presence and mids.
Features
Gain with two stages: one gain control with a high/low switch, from crunchy rock to full saturation
Classic control set: bass, middle, treble, presence, level and master
Built-in gate: input, gate and output right in the plugin, for clean pauses at high gain
Cabinet included: built-in cabinet simulation, or load your own impulse responses
Standalone with ASIO: play without a DAW, using your interface's ASIO driver or ASIO4ALL
VST3: for every DAW that loads VST3 plugins, and for Dual Amp Loader
System requirements
Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
A DAW with VST3 support, or the standalone version
Audio interface with ASIO driver recommended
Installation
Download the latest file from Releases.
VST3: copy `Pyroclast.vst3` to `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3` and let your DAW rescan its plugins.
Standalone: start `Pyroclast.exe` and choose your audio interface in the audio settings.
